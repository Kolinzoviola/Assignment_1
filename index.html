Biography

My name is Emeka Collins Elumelu, but most people call me Collins. I am a doctor. I work as a Consultant Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgeon in Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery at University College Hospital (UCH), Ibadan, Nigeria. I take care of patients with broken bones, bone infections and other bone problems.

In the hospital, I see that many records are still kept on paper. Sometimes patient information gets lost, and it takes a long time to find things. This made me interested in technology. I want to build simple computer tools that can help doctors and patients.

I have started learning to code, and I enjoy sharing what I learn with other people.

Why I Joined AltSchool Africa

I joined AltSchool Africa for these reasons:
